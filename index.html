✨ Traveler Questions (FAQ)

Can I customize the day-by-day itinerary times? 🌸

Yes! When you submit the "Plan Trip" form with your chosen destination, travel pace, and travel style, TravelMate automatically crafts an aligned morning, afternoon, and evening schedule.

How does the packing list track my progress? 🧳

Each checkbox updates your suitcase readiness progress bar in real time, and you can add unlimited custom sticker items to any category.

Can I calculate in different currencies? 💰

Yes! Switch between USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), INR (₹), or AUD (A$) anytime using the budget dropdown.
